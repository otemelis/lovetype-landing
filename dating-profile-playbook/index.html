The through-line for each LoveType

The Quiet Builder

Show, don't announce. Your appeal is evidence, not adjectives — let the specifics of how you actually live do the work.

Starter line: “Same Sunday hike for six years, rain or not. Looking for someone worth slowing it down for.”

About The Quiet Builder →

The Loyal Reassurer

Lead with how present you are — that's the gift. Just frame it as devotion, not as a list of requirements.

Starter line: “When I'm with someone, I'm actually with them. Phone down, fully here.”

About The Loyal Reassurer →

The Independent Romantic

Be upfront that you want a partner, not a merger. Anyone who'd chafe at that screens themselves out — which is the point.

Starter line: “I disappear into the darkroom most weekends and come back better company for it.”

About The Independent Romantic →

The Guarded Heart

You don't owe a dating profile your history. Lead with what you're moving toward, not what you're recovering from.

Starter line: “Ask me about tide pools. I will not stop.”

About The Guarded Heart →

The Warm Nurturer

Show the warmth — it's your whole appeal — but make sure the profile also says you want to be cared for, not only to give.

Starter line: “You mention once that you love a certain snack, and it's in my bag next time. That's just how I run.”

About The Warm Nurturer →

The Tender Presence

Physical warmth is how you connect — say so directly, but keep it about closeness and presence, not just chemistry.

Starter line: “I'm a hand-on-the-back, sit-close-on-the-couch person. Affection isn't rationed.”

About The Tender Presence →

The Thoughtful Partner

Your edge is attention to detail — so make the profile itself specific. Vague profiles undersell you badly.

Starter line: “I'll remember the name of your childhood dog and bring it up a year later.”

About The Thoughtful Partner →

The Joyful Explorer

Lead with momentum and a concrete plan — but signal you can also do a quiet Tuesday, or you'll only attract people who can't sit still either.

Starter line: “I have a list of 30 diners across the state and I've done 11. Come do the other 19 with me.”

About The Joyful Explorer →

The Deep Processor

Name that you're a slow, considered communicator. It's a real compatibility filter, and the right person reads it as depth.

Starter line: “I rarely have the perfect response in the moment. Give me a day and I'll come back with something real.”

About The Deep Processor →

The Passionate Advocate

Own the intensity — it's your best quality — and pair it with a line that shows you can also let someone breathe.

Starter line: “I don't do lukewarm. If I'm in, I'm in, and you'll never wonder where you stand.”

About The Passionate Advocate →

Questions

How do I write a dating profile that sounds like me?

Lead with one through-line, the quality people actually experience with you, and prove it with a specific detail instead of an adjective. Your LoveType gives you that through-line; the playbook turns it into lines you can use.

What should I avoid in a dating profile?

Lists of adjectives, logistics with no warmth, and lines anyone could have written. Each LoveType has its own typical trap; the full playbook in the app names yours.

Does the playbook write the profile for me?

No. It gives you guidance, starter lines and ideas for common prompts, so the profile stays in your voice.
