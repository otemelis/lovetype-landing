LoveType is a relationship self-reflection app. This policy explains what stays on your iPhone, the little that doesn’t, and why.

The short version

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your answers stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us.
  • We collect anonymous usage events to understand how the app is used, including which result type you received.
  • We don’t sell data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

What stays on your device

Your answers to every test, your results, your LoveType profile and any first name you choose to enter are stored only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Reset all test results, or by deleting the app.

What we collect

When you use LoveType, the app records anonymous usage events, for example that a test was started or completed, which result type you received (such as “The Peacekeeper”), that a screen was viewed, or that a purchase was completed. Each event carries:

  • a random installation ID created on your device, and a random session ID;
  • the app version, the iOS version and the platform;
  • the app language and the region set on your device (country level only);
  • the time of the event.

Events never include your answers, your name, your email, your contacts or your precise location. The installation ID is not linked to your Apple ID or to any other identity, and it is reset if you delete the app.

Partner comparison

If you use Compare with a partner, the app shows you a four-character code. The code contains only your three result types (attachment, love and conflict style) and nothing else. It is shared only if you share it, and comparing happens on each person’s own phone. On love-type.app, a code you type into the compare page is read in your browser and is not sent to us.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback through the app, the text you write is stored with your rating and result type so we can read it. Please don’t include personal details.

Purchases

The full LoveType Guide is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles all payments; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm the purchase and restore it on your devices, using an anonymous ID created by RevenueCat.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores anonymous usage events and optional feedback on our behalf.
  • RevenueCat manages purchase status and restores.
  • Apple processes payments and, if you opt in to sharing with developers, provides crash reports and anonymous App Store analytics.

We don’t sell or rent data to anyone, and we don’t use it for advertising.

This website

love-type.app is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies. To understand which pages help people find the app, the site sends a few anonymous events to the same Supabase service: the page viewed, the referring website, whether an App Store button was tapped, your browser language and a rough device type (phone, tablet or desktop). Each event carries a random visit ID kept in your browser’s session storage, so we can see how people move between pages during one visit. Your browser deletes it when you close the tab, and it is replaced after 30 minutes without activity, so separate visits can’t be linked to each other or to you. Your IP address is not stored. There are no advertising or third-party tracking scripts. GitHub may process standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

Your choices and rights

You can delete everything stored on your device from Settings in the app. Because the events we collect are anonymous and not linked to you, we can’t find a specific person’s events on request; there is nothing in them that identifies you. For any privacy question or request, contact us at the address below.

Children

LoveType is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13.
