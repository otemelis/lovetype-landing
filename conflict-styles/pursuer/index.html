Conflict style·The Pursuer

The Pursuer.You need resolution to feel safe — silence isn't an option.

Take the conflict style test
In shortA pursuer is someone who needs to address a conflict right away. Unresolved tension feels unsafe to them, so they push to talk it through now, and a partner going quiet can feel like love being withdrawn.

Signs of a pursuer in conflict

  • Wants to resolve a fight before sleeping
  • Follows a partner who walks away
  • Feels silence as rejection
  • Raises issues directly

How LoveType describes it

If this is your result, LoveType calls you The Pursuer.

When something feels wrong, you need to address it — now. Letting tension sit unresolved is more painful to you than the conflict itself. You pursue connection through conversation, and when someone goes quiet, it can feel like they're withdrawing their love, not just taking space.

Strengths

  • Emotionally brave
  • Doesn't let problems fester
  • Keeps relationships honest and clear
  • Committed to working things out
Worth knowingPursuing too hard when a partner needs space can trigger more withdrawal — a painful cycle where you both get less of what you need.

Questions

How can a pursuer stop chasing during conflict?

Say what you need once, clearly, then let the pause happen. Agreeing on a time to come back makes waiting much easier.

Is being a pursuer the same as anxious attachment?

They often appear together, but they measure different things. Attachment is about closeness in general; conflict style is about what you do during a disagreement.
