Quality time.Being chosen is the deepest love.

Take the love style test
In shortQuality time is the love style of people who feel most loved through undivided attention. What matters is not how many hours, but whether a partner is fully present: phone down, listening, choosing to be there.

Signs of a quality time love style

  • Values long talks and unhurried evenings
  • Feels hurt by distracted attention
  • Remembers moments more than gifts
  • Plans time together on purpose

How LoveType describes it

If this is your result, LoveType calls you The Presence Lover.

What you crave most is presence — real, unhurried, undivided attention. It's not about how much time someone spends with you, but whether they're truly there when they are. A partner who puts the phone down and chooses you fully, even for an hour, means more than grand gestures ever could.

Strengths

  • Deeply attentive in relationships
  • Values quality over performance
  • Creates space for real connection
  • Doesn't need much to feel loved — just genuine presence
Worth knowingYou may feel unloved during busy seasons, even when a partner is showing care in other ways — learning to receive love through action or words helps bridge that gap.

Questions

How do you show love to a quality time person?

Protect time that is only for them and be fully there in it. Twenty attentive minutes count for more than an evening spent half on a screen.

Is quality time the same as spending a lot of time together?

No. Quantity helps less than presence. Feeling chosen and listened to is the point.
