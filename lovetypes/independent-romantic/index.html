The Independent Romantic.You choose connection — on your own terms.

Is this my LoveType?

You're not someone who needs a relationship. You want one — and that distinction is meaningful to you. You bring calm, self-sufficiency, and a quiet kind of loyalty. You love in spaces and adventures rather than in constant togetherness, and you need a partner who respects that your independence isn't distance — it's how you stay whole.

Most often built from

Attachment: avoidant. Love style: shared experiences or words of affirmation. Conflict style: withdrawer or defender.

LoveType weighs all three of your results together, so other combinations can arrive here as well.

Strengths

  • Brings calm and stability
  • Never clingy — loves with intention
  • Keeps a clear sense of self within relationships
  • Creates space for both people to remain individuals
Worth knowingYour emotional availability can be hard to read, leaving partners uncertain whether you're deeply engaged or simply comfortable with less.

On a dating profile

From the dating-profile playbook for The Independent Romantic. The through-line to lead with:

Be upfront that you want a partner, not a merger. Anyone who'd chafe at that screens themselves out — which is the point.

Worth putting in: A pursuit that's fully your own. It signals you'll bring a whole life to a relationship, not a vacancy.

Starter line: “I disappear into the darkroom most weekends and come back better company for it.”

Questions

What is The Independent Romantic LoveType?

The Independent Romantic is one of LoveType’s ten relationship archetypes: “You choose connection — on your own terms.” It comes from combining three results: attachment style, love style and conflict style.

Which results lead to The Independent Romantic?

Most often avoidant attachment, a love style of shared experiences or words of affirmation, and a withdrawer or defender conflict style. Other combinations can land here too, because all three results are weighed together.
