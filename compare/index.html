How it worksThree steps, two phones.

  1. Both take the three tests. About eleven minutes each, free.
  2. Share your code. LoveType gives each of you four characters, like 10E7.
  3. Enter theirs. You both see where you click, where you grind and one thing each of you can try, for attachment, love and conflict.

Questions

How do I get my LoveType code?

Finish all three tests in LoveType (attachment, love style and conflict style). Your four-character code then appears under Compare with a partner, with a button to share it.

What does the code reveal?

Only your three result types. No answers, no name, no scores. Anyone with the code can see those three types, so share it with people you choose.

Is there a compatibility score?

No, on purpose. LoveType shows where two styles tend to click, where they tend to grind, and one thing each person can try. Scores turn a pattern into a verdict.

Does comparing cost anything?

No. Comparing with a partner is free for both of you.
