Compatibility

How the styles pair up

Every combination of attachment, love and conflict style has a shape: somewhere the two people fit without trying, and somewhere they predictably grind.

These are patterns, not scores. There is no percentage and no ranking here — a "hard" pairing is not a doomed one, and an "easy" pairing still takes effort. Each page covers where a combination connects, where the friction shows up, and one concrete thing each partner can try.
