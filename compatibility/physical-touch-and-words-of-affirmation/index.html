Physical Touch and Words of Affirmation.

Love both felt and heard — the hand on the shoulder and the sentence in the same evening.

Love style describes how a person gives and recognises affection. It is not a ranking of couples, and every combination can work.

The two styles

Physical Touch: Connects through physical closeness — a hand on the back, a long hug, sitting close. Affection is not optional; it is how they know they are wanted.

Words of Affirmation: Gives and receives love through language — saying it, out loud and specifically. Silence where words should be can feel like absence.

Where you connect

One partner reaches for the other; the other tells them. Between them love is both felt and heard — the hand on the shoulder and the 'I'm glad you're mine' in the same evening.

Where the friction shows up

The touch partner shows love by closing the physical distance; the words partner shows it by saying it. One can find the need to verbalise everything a lot; the other can feel wanted near without ever being told why.

What each of you can try

If you are the physical touch partnerPut a few words to the affection — 'I love being close to you.' The touch plus the sentence reaches them twice.
If you are the words of affirmation partnerWhen they reach for you, that is the sentence. Lean in instead of waiting for them to say it.
A pattern, not a verdictStyles describe tendencies, not destinies. Plenty of couples with this pairing do well; knowing the pattern is what makes it workable.

Questions

Are physical touch and words of affirmation compatible?

They complement each other — one is felt, the other heard — and each is quick to add to the other. The pairing works once the touch partner offers the occasional sentence and the words partner accepts a reach as a statement.

My partner is very physical but rarely says loving things — what does that mean?

For a touch-oriented partner, the reach is the message. If you need it in words too, ask directly; pairing a sentence with the contact they already offer is usually an easy adjustment for them.
