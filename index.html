Questions

Before you start

What is LoveType?

LoveType is a relationship self-reflection app with three short tests — Attachment Style, Love Style, and Conflict Style — that combine into one LoveType profile. It's built for personal insight, not clinical diagnosis.

Is LoveType free?

Yes. All three tests are free to take. A one-time purchase unlocks the full LoveType Guide — deeper breakdowns, your combined archetype, and personalized scripts. There's no subscription.

Does LoveType store my quiz answers?

No. Your answers and results are stored only on your device, in the app's private local storage. They are never transmitted to our servers. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Is an attachment style test the same as a love language quiz?

No — they measure different things. Attachment style describes how you seek closeness, trust, and security in relationships. Love style (love language) describes how you give and receive affection. LoveType measures both, plus how you handle conflict, so you get a fuller picture than either test alone.

Is LoveType a substitute for therapy?

No. LoveType is a self-reflection tool, not a medical device, and doesn't provide therapy, counselling, or diagnosis. If you're going through a difficult time, please reach out to a qualified professional.
