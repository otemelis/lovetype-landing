Love style·The Word Lover

Words of affirmation.Words are how love becomes real.

Take the love style test
In shortWords of affirmation is the love style of people who feel most loved through what is said: compliments, appreciation, encouragement and hearing “I love you” out loud. For them, specific words make love real, and silence where words should be can feel like distance.

Signs of a words of affirmation love style

  • Remembers kind things people said years ago
  • Feels hurt by harsh words more than most
  • Expresses love by saying it
  • Notices when appreciation goes unspoken

How LoveType describes it

If this is your result, LoveType calls you The Word Lover.

Language is your love currency. You give love by expressing it — out loud, in messages, in all the small ways words can land. And you receive love most deeply when someone tells you, specifically, that you matter to them. Silence where words should be can feel like absence.

Strengths

  • Expressive and affirming
  • Makes others feel truly seen and heard
  • Creates emotional clarity in relationships
  • Creates emotional safety through language
Worth knowingYou may over-rely on words and miss the love that's being shown through actions or presence — especially with partners who express differently.

Questions

How do you show love to someone with words of affirmation?

Be specific and say it unprompted: what you noticed, what you admire, what they did that mattered. A short message in the middle of the day often lands better than a big speech.

What hurts someone whose love language is words?

Criticism, sarcasm and long silences. Withheld appreciation can feel like withheld love.
