Love style·The Memory Maker

Shared experiences.Memories are how love becomes permanent.

Take the love style test
In shortShared experiences is the love style of people who feel closest while doing something together: a trip, a concert, a new restaurant, a project. For them, love lives in memories made side by side, and routine without anything new can feel like drifting apart.

Signs of a shared experiences love style

  • Plans adventures and new things to try
  • Bonds through doing rather than talking
  • Remembers relationships by their moments
  • Feels restless when life gets repetitive

How LoveType describes it

If this is your result, LoveType calls you The Memory Maker.

Love, for you, lives in shared moments. A trip, a concert, something you'd never have done alone — these become the architecture of a relationship. You feel closest to people during experiences, not in side-by-side daily routine. You want someone who says 'let's go' — who builds a life made of things worth remembering.

Strengths

  • Brings joy and adventure into relationships
  • Creates lasting memories
  • Keeps love feeling alive and forward-moving
  • Naturally pulls people out of their comfort zone
Worth knowingYou may undervalue the quiet, ordinary moments — which are often where the deepest relationships are built.

Questions

Is shared experiences one of the five love languages?

It isn’t one of the original five. LoveType adds it as a sixth love style because many people feel most connected through doing new things together.

How do you love someone who values shared experiences?

Make plans together and protect them: small adventures count, like a new walk or cooking something neither of you has tried.
