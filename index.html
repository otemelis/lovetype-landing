Before you start

What is LoveType?

LoveType is an iPhone app with three short relationship tests: Attachment Style, Love Style and Conflict Style. Together they take about eleven minutes and combine into one LoveType, a profile of how you bond, how you give and feel love, and what you do when things get tense.

Is LoveType free?

Yes. All three tests and your basic results are free, with no account. A single one-time purchase unlocks the full guide: every deep dive, your Love Manual, communication scripts and the complete dating-profile playbook. There is no subscription.

How does the partner comparison work?

Once you have finished all three tests, LoveType gives you a four-character code. Share it with a partner; when they enter it in their app, you both see where you click, where you tend to grind and one thing each of you can try. There is no compatibility score, and comparing is free.

What is the dating-profile playbook?

It turns your LoveType into guidance for writing a dating profile that sounds like you: the through-line to lead with, what to mention, starter lines and ideas for common prompts. Part of it is free; the rest comes with the full guide.

What is a Love Manual?

Four short lines, written from your three results, that tell someone how to love you: when you feel loved, when you feel unsafe, what happens to you under pressure and the best way to repair. It is made to be shared with someone you trust.

Is an attachment style test the same as a love language quiz?

No. Attachment style is about how you seek closeness and safety; love style (often called love language) is about how you give and recognise affection. LoveType measures both, plus your conflict style, because the three explain each other.

Do my answers leave my phone?

No. Your answers and results are stored on your iPhone. LoveType sends anonymous usage events to improve the app, including which result type you received, but never your answers, your name or your email.

Which languages is LoveType available in?

English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Traditional Chinese and Indonesian. You can switch language in Settings.

Is LoveType therapy?

No. LoveType is a self-reflection tool, not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional help. If you are going through something difficult, please talk to a qualified professional.
