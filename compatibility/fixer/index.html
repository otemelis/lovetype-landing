Conflict style pairing

Two Fixer Partners.

Two action-oriented partners who can solve the symptom while the real hurt goes unspoken.

Conflict style describes what a person does when a disagreement gets difficult. It is a tendency, not a fault, and no combination is doomed.

The fixer style

Fixer: Wants to identify the issue, propose a solution and get to the other side. Sitting in the feeling without working toward a fix is hard; fixing is how their care shows up. In this pairing both people bring that same set of strengths, and the same blind spots.

Where you connect

Both partners are action-oriented and neither wallows. A problem shows up and both move toward it — plans, options, next steps. Nothing sits unresolved for long.

Where the friction shows up

Both can be so busy solving that neither slows down to feel what is actually wrong. Two people fixing the symptom while the real issue — the hurt underneath — goes unspoken.

What each of you can try

For both partnersBefore you build the fix, ask each other: 'do you want a solution right now, or do you want to be heard first?' Sometimes the answer is neither, and you both need to just sit with it.
A pattern, not a verdictStyles describe tendencies, not destinies. Plenty of couples with this pairing do well; knowing the pattern is what makes it workable.

Questions

Is it a problem if both partners just want to fix things?

Only when the fixing skips the feeling. Practical problems get solved efficiently, but the emotional layer can go unaddressed for years. Occasionally asking whether the moment calls for a solution or just for listening keeps that from happening.
